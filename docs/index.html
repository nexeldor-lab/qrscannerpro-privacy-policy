1. What Data We Collect

The Short Answer: We collect NO personal information and NO device data.

QRScannerPro does not collect, store, or transmit any:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers (device ID, IMEI, MAC address, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Analytics or usage data
  • Crash reports or performance data
  • Advertising data

2. How the App Works

When you use QRScannerPro, you can:

  • Scan QR codes containing WiFi details
  • Generate QR codes with WiFi information

All WiFi details from scanned or generated QR codes are stored exclusively on your device in your phone's local storage. This data never leaves your phone.

3. Data Storage

  • Where: All data is stored locally on your Android device only
  • Who can access: Only you, through QRScannerPro
  • Cloud sync: None - your data never goes to our servers or anywhere else
  • Deletion: You can delete any data anytime through the app's data management features

4. No Third-Party Services

QRScannerPro does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Crash reporting tools
  • Advertising networks
  • Cloud storage services
  • Any other third-party data processors

5. Your Rights

Data Access

You have full access to any data stored in QRScannerPro on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete any WiFi details or generated QR codes at any time through the app.

Data Portability

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you maintain complete control and portability of your information.

6. Security

While we don't collect data, the security of your local device is important. We recommend:

  • Keeping your Android device and QRScannerPro updated
  • Using device-level security features (screen lock, encryption)
  • Backing up important WiFi QR codes if needed

7. Children's Privacy

QRScannerPro is designed for general use. Since we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child's data has been collected, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

9. Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
  • Google Play Store privacy requirements
