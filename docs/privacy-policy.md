# Privacy Policy for QRScannerPro

**Last Updated:** May 22, 2026

## Overview

QRScannerPro is designed with privacy as a core principle. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our Android application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data, including information about our free version with ads and the ad-free Pro version. This policy also covers our integration of Google Safe Browsing API for malware detection.

## 1. What Data We Collect

**The Short Answer:** We collect NO personal information and NO device data for our own purposes.

QRScannerPro does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following to our servers:
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Device identifiers (device ID, IMEI, MAC address, etc.)
- Location data
- Scanning history or QR code content
- Analytics or usage data
- Crash reports or performance data

### Important Exceptions - Third-Party Services:

#### 1. **Google Mobile Ads (Free Version Only)**
In the free version of QRScannerPro, we use Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google may collect:
- Advertising ID
- Installation source
- Device information (model, OS version)
- General location (city/country level, not precise)
- Demographic information (age range, gender, interests)

**Note:** Ads do NOT appear in the Pro version. Users who purchase the Pro version enjoy a completely ad-free experience.

For more information about Google's ad practices, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

#### 2. **Google Safe Browsing API (All Versions)**
QRScannerPro uses Google's Safe Browsing API to protect users from malware and phishing threats:
- When you scan a QR code containing a URL, the app checks that URL against Google's database of known threats
- This check is performed in real-time for immediate protection
- Google may receive:
  - The URL being checked
  - Your general location (derived from IP address, city/country level)
  - Device type and OS version
  - Safe Browsing service logs and diagnostics

**Important:** Only the URL itself is shared with Google's Safe Browsing API. Your identity, other scanned codes, or personal data are NOT sent to Google for this check.

For more information about Google Safe Browsing, visit: https://safebrowsing.google.com/

## 2. How the App Works

When you use QRScannerPro, you can:
- **Scan QR codes** containing WiFi details, URLs, contact information, and more
- **Generate QR codes** with WiFi information, contact details, and custom data

All data from scanned or generated QR codes is stored **exclusively on your device** in your phone's local storage. This data never leaves your phone, except when:
- You explicitly export your history (which you control)
- URLs are checked against Google Safe Browsing API for threat detection (see Section 1)
- Ads are served in the free version

## 3. Data Storage

- **Where:** All user-created data is stored locally on your Android device only
- **Who can access:** Only you, through QRScannerPro
- **Cloud sync:** None - your scanning history and QR codes never sync to cloud servers
- **Deletion:** You can delete any data anytime through the app's data management features

## 4. Free Version vs. Pro Version

### Free Version
- **Advertisements:** Includes Google Mobile Ads displayed in the app
- **Features:** All core QR scanning and generation features included
- **Safe Browsing:** Includes URL threat detection via Google Safe Browsing API
- **Ad Data:** Google may collect anonymized advertising data (see Section 1)

### Pro Version (One-Time Purchase)
- **Advertisements:** Completely ad-free - NO ads are displayed
- **Features:** All core features + exclusive Pro features (templates, customization, batch mode, app lock)
- **Safe Browsing:** Includes URL threat detection via Google Safe Browsing API (same as free version)
- **Ad Data:** No advertising networks involved - only Safe Browsing API used for threat detection

## 5. Third-Party Services Summary

QRScannerPro uses the following third-party services:

### Services Used in Free Version Only
- **Google Mobile Ads** (advertising network) - displays ads and collects advertising data

### Services Used in Both Free and Pro Versions
- **Google Safe Browsing API** - real-time malware and phishing detection
- **Google Play Billing** - for in-app purchase transactions (Pro version purchase)
- **Google Play Services** - core Android functionality (location, device info)
- **Google ML Kit** - barcode/QR code detection and recognition

QRScannerPro does NOT use:
- Analytics services (Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, etc.)
- Crash reporting tools
- User behavior tracking networks
- Cloud storage services
- Email or SMS services

## 6. Your Rights

### Data Access
You have full access to any data stored in QRScannerPro on your device through the app's interface.

### Data Deletion
You can delete any WiFi details, contact information, generated QR codes, or scans at any time through the app's data management features.

### Data Portability
Since all data is stored locally on your device, you maintain complete control and can export your data via the app's export features (CSV/JSON formats).

### Ad Preferences (Free Version)
You can manage your advertising preferences through:
- Google Settings → Ads → Manage your Google Ad Personalization
- Device Settings → Apps & Permissions → Permissions → disable tracking

### Opting Out of Ads
The easiest way to opt out of seeing ads is to upgrade to the Pro version through the app.

## 7. Security

While we don't collect personal data, the security of your device and data is important:
- QRScannerPro uses Google Safe Browsing to warn about malicious URLs
- We recommend keeping your Android device and QRScannerPro updated
- Use device-level security features (screen lock, encryption)
- Back up important WiFi QR codes if needed
- Be cautious when using open WiFi networks
